About the studio

Small team. Big glow-ups.

Hey Gorgeous is a neighborhood beauty studio built around one idea — that getting your hair, nails and skin done should feel like the best part of your week, not a rushed errand.

We keep the team small so every appointment is unhurried, every consult is real, and every stylist actually knows your name. Clean product lines, honest pricing, no upsell theatre.

  • 6 stylists on the floor
  • 20 min built-in consult on every first visit
  • Cruelty-free product line, top to bottom
