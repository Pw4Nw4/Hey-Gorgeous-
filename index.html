The trial

How a trial actually goes.

It’s part haircare test-drive, part creative consult, part quiet afternoon — and it’s the single most useful hour of the planning process.

  1. 01

    Consult

    A relaxed call or studio visit about 10–14 weeks out. We talk dress, venue, light, skin & hair history, and pull inspiration together.

  2. 02

    Trial session

    Two hours, ~6–8 weeks before the wedding. Full hair and makeup, photos in natural light, tweak anything that isn’t quite right. Bring your veil and any hair pieces.

  3. 03

    Wedding day

    I arrive with a locked schedule and detailed notes from the trial — so the morning is about you and your people, not decisions.

FAQ

The usual questions.

If yours isn’t here, drop it in the inquiry form and I’ll answer in the reply.

What does travel cost?

Up to 60 miles from the studio is included. Beyond that it’s $0.75/mile round-trip, plus lodging for anything over a 90-minute drive or a pre-8am start. Destination weddings quote separately.

How do deposits and cancellations work?

A 25% retainer holds your date. The balance is due two weeks before the wedding. If you cancel more than 90 days out, the retainer rolls to a studio credit valid for 12 months.

Is the trial included?

Yes — every package includes a full 2-hour trial. Additional trials (for a second-look or if you’ve changed direction) are $175.

What’s in the touch-up kit?

Your exact lipstick, a blotting paper, a mini setting spray, extra bobby pins in your hair color, and a safety-pin & bandage combo because someone always needs one.

What if it rains / the schedule slips?

The getting-ready timeline has 20 minutes of built-in buffer for exactly this. For outdoor ceremonies, I stay through first looks and bring waterproof everything for the bride.

How should I prep my skin and hair?

You’ll get a two-page prep guide after your trial. Short version: no new products in the two weeks before, hydrate like it’s your job, and wash your hair the night before — not the morning of.

Can my headcount change after booking?

Up to 30 days out, yes — subject to artist availability. Beyond that, adds may require a second artist, billed at cost.
